Privacy & credits

What this app stores

  • On your device only: your history of anagrams (the letters, how you arranged them, locked boxes, word lengths) and your light/dark choice. This is kept in your browser's local storage so the app can show it again next time. It is never sent to a server, and clearing the history in the app (or your browser's site data) removes it.
  • Offline copy: the app and its word lists are saved in your browser's cache so it works without a connection.
  • No cookies, no accounts, no ads. The letters you type are processed entirely on your device.

Visitor statistics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits (page views, country, device type, referring site and page-load timings). Cloudflare states that it does not track individual visitors across sites. This app sets no cookies of its own, and the analytics never see the letters you enter or your history.
